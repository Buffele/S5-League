# Imported nature assets

Trees, flowering shrubs, fern and rocks: **Quaternius**, Stylized Nature MegaKit.

Source: https://poly.pizza/bundle/Stylized-Nature-MegaKit-T34GZFA0fm

License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Models are scaled, rotated and batched into the map. Materials are adapted
for nonmetallic surfaces and cutout foliage; runtime adds subtle leaf wind.
Individual source URLs and original-file SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
`art/circle2-imports/sources.json` in the source repository.

Architecture, signage and paving: original project assets. Current sky:
anime illustration regenerated using the explicitly authorized ImageGen
fallback CLI/API (`gpt-image-2`, high quality), from the previous anime sky.
Native size: 3840 x 1920; no local upscaling.
Exact prompt: `art/circle2-atlas/sky-anime-4k-prompt.txt`.
Provenance: `art/circle2-atlas/sky-anime-4k-provenance.json`.

## Rejected photographic sky — not used

**Kloppenheim 03 (Pure Sky)** by Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, Poly Haven.

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_03_puresky

License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The original 8192 x 4096 tonemapped JPG was tried, then rejected as inconsistent
with the anime art direction. It is not loaded or shipped in the game assets.
Source hash and URL remain in `art/circle2-atlas/sky-8k-source.json`.

Kenney Space Kit is not used in this map.

## Observation probes

Drone probe sphere (UFO Crash Site and Recovery Facility), by **3D Assets**.
The publisher identifies this asset as made with AI tools.

Source: https://3dassets.dev/assets/ufo-crash-site-and-recovery-facility-drone-probe-spher-b0489160

License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Adaptations: ivory/slate palette, opaque sensor glass, material roughness,
normalization, mesh batching and three animated runtime flight paths.
Download URL and SHA-256: `art/circle2-imports/probe-source.json`.
